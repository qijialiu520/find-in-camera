Find in Camera

Privacy Policy

Updated November 23, 2026

The short version: Find in Camera processes camera frames on your device and does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data. Capture saves a photo to your Photos library when you choose.

Data collection

Find in Camera has no account system, advertising, analytics, tracking, or third-party SDKs. Qijia Liu does not receive your camera images, recognized text, search queries, device identifiers, location, contacts, or usage information.

Camera and text recognition

The app requests camera permission to recognize and highlight matching text on a physical page. Camera frames are processed temporarily on your device using Apple’s Vision framework. When you tap Capture, the app creates an image with the matching text highlighted and adds it to your Photos library. The app never uploads camera images and does not store recognized text or search queries separately.

Permissions

Camera access is required for the core scanning feature. You can revoke access at any time in iOS Settings. The app requests add-only Photos permission the first time you use Capture. This permission lets the app save your captures and provides no access to photos already in your library. You can manage both permissions in iOS Settings.

Local settings

Your selected recognition language is saved in the app’s local settings so it can be restored the next time you open Find in Camera. This preference is not sent off your device and is removed when you delete the app.

Data retention and deletion

You can manage or delete captured images in Photos. Your Apple Photos and iCloud settings govern how Photos syncs those images. Find in Camera does not retain a separate copy, and Qijia Liu receives no personal data to retain or delete.

Children’s privacy

The app is suitable for a general audience and does not knowingly collect information from children or anyone else.

Changes

If this policy changes, the effective date above will be updated and the revised policy will be made available on this page and inside the app.
